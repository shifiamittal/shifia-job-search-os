## Keystone.ai · Principal Product Manager – Technical
Jun 2024 – Present · Seattle, WA

Keystone.ai builds Deep Enterprise, an agentic AI platform that turns supply chain planning decisions into AI workflows for Fortune 500 manufacturers and retailers. I own product strategy, roadmap and go-to-market, partnering with the CEO and CTO and setting direction for 20+ engineers and data scientists.

• Led Keystone's pivot from consulting to a product-led B2B SaaS model: $5M ARR, $25M in annualized client savings, live with 5 enterprise customers.
• Took Deep Enterprise from 0→1 to production: improved forecast accuracy (WAPE) 21% and cut enterprise deployment from 6 months to 4.
• Prototyped and shipped a multi-agent forecasting system that flags exceptions, diagnoses root causes with tools, and recommends retrain or override actions, using RAG, model routing, human-in-the-loop approval and risk-tiered autonomy. Planner effort fell from 4 hours a day to 4 hours a week.
• Set up AI evaluation and governance for agents: task correctness, groundedness, retrieval quality, tool compliance, action safety and regression testing, with automated checks and human review before rollout.
• Built a semantic layer, data catalog and metadata generation to ground agents in client data. Agent recommendations reached 70% acceptance; model routing cut agent cost ~30%.
• Designed a multi-tenant data platform (ingestion, transformation, validation, lineage, model-ready APIs) that cut deployment from 3 months to 3 weeks and saved data scientists ~2 weeks per model.
• Launched HAT-AI, a generative AI assistant for roadmap reporting and knowledge discovery used by 30 PMs across 15 finance products (120 hours saved a week), and REI-Agent, a conversational multi-agent assistant for financial analysis with governed metrics and auditable calculations.

Skills: Agentic AI · AI Agents · Generative AI · Large Language Models (LLM) · Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) · AI Evaluation · Workflow Automation · Data Platforms · Product Strategy · Go-to-Market Strategy
