## 3. What the data says

1. **The round almost always opens with "3 favorite products"** (15 of 37 entries). Have 3 products ready, each with: who it's for, why it works, one thing you'd improve, and how you'd adapt it for a new user group. Expect the interviewer to pick, not you.
2. **Since 2025 the main question is "Design [famous app] for [niche user]."** 12 of the 18 entries from 2025–2026: Spotify for restaurants, Airbnb for pets, Maps for hikers, Slack for plant lovers, WhatsApp for daycares, Netflix podcasts, adapt for kids/elderly, expand to a new user base.
3. **Repeats are real.** Spotify for restaurants (3×, latest 08/31/2026), Airbnb for pets (2× in 2026), Maps for hikers (2× in 2026), airport ATM (5×, latest 08/06/2026). Practice these four word for word.
4. **No payments questions.** None of the 37 is about Stripe's products. The fraud-detection prompts in `insider-data/company-intel/stripe.md` don't appear here; use them for execution and "why Stripe" prep, not this round.
5. **No pricing questions.** 0 of 37. Growth shows up only as Google Maps SMB engagement (4×). Launch shows up once (digitize smell). For Stripe, spend practice time on Design and Improvement first; pricing last.
6. **Older (2021–2022) prompts were more open-ended** (air travel perception, Disneyland lines, Domino's). Less likely now, but one practice run on a "big ambiguous problem" is cheap insurance.
