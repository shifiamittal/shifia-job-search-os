## Patterns in this snapshot

- **Strongest fits on paper** (AI scope + your strengths, at Principal/Staff level):
  - ServiceNow Staff TPM, AI Evaluation Science: evals are your core differentiator (Keystone eval framework: groundedness, tool compliance, action safety).
  - Palo Alto Networks Principal PM, Prisma AIRS: AI security connects to your action-safety and governance work.
  - Amazon Principal PMT – AI, Smart Commerce: AI plus your Amazon seller/commerce background.
  - Backbase (Agentic) PM and IDFC FIRST Lead AI PM: agents/AI in banking, close to your credit and fintech work (both posted below Principal).
- **Level check:** 9 of 25 are below Principal/Staff (PM, Lead). In India, "Lead PM" can be senior, but compare against the ₹1.0 Cr suggested floor once you see the JD or band.
- **Cities outside your salary research:** Pune (3 roles) and Mumbai (2 roles). `salary-research-india.md` only covers Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
- **AI scope unclear on 13 roles.** Microsoft (2), Deloitte Director, WhatsApp Director, Intuit, Target, Cognite and Cornerstone need a JD check before they count.
