- **Big Tech dominates volume.** Amazon, Microsoft, Walmart, and Meta account for 11 of 25 roles.

  - *Personalization & discovery:* Amazon Personalization, Prime Video Personalization ML, Netflix Personalization, Nordstrom Discovery.
  - *Platform / infra:* Walmart Platform, Pulumi IaC, Auger Platform Experience, Meta AI Infra, Snowflake Transactions.
- **Warmest paths (by connection count):** Expedia (36), Microsoft (27), Salesforce (26), AWS (15), Meta (14), Snowflake (10).
- **Highest posted comp:** Snowflake Director ($304K–$437K), Meta Director ($249K–$323K), Expedia Principal ($224K–$313.5K).
- **Not on target list yet:** Meta, Walmart, Netflix, Nordstrom, JPMorganChase, Axon, Pulumi, Auger, Synthesia, EY-Parthenon.
