- **Amazon is 11 of 48 roles** even though you are not pursuing it. Treat it as a market signal for what Seattle PM work looks like, not a target.
- **Agentic AI is the dominant theme.** 13 roles: Microsoft Cowork/Autopilot, AWS Velocity Labs (x2), AWS Amazon Quick, Amazon AI Customer Service, Google GDC Agentic Platforms, Stripe Agentic Commerce, JPMC Agent Services + Agent Core, DigitalOcean Agentic AI Infra, Storable AI Agents, Meta AI Platforms & Infra, Synthesia.

- **Personalization & discovery:** Amazon, Prime Video (x2), Netflix, Nordstrom, Walmart Insights & Recs.
- **Warmest paths (by connection count):** Expedia (36), Microsoft (27), Salesforce (26), Stripe (19), Google (18), Uber (18), AWS (15), Meta (14).
- **Top-5 targets with open roles right now:** Google #3 (Kirkland), Microsoft #4 (3 roles), Salesforce #5.
- **Highest posted comp:** Snowflake Director ($304K–$437K), Meta Director ($249K–$323K), Expedia Principal ($224K–$313.5K).
- **Not on target list yet:** Meta, Walmart, Netflix, Nordstrom, JPMorganChase, Alaska Airlines, F5, DigitalOcean, Storable, Axon, Pulumi, Auger, Synthesia, EY-Parthenon.
