AI skills are a plus in these roles, not the reason to apply.

## Gates (updated 2026-10-20)

A role must pass both:
1. **Location:** Seattle area (on-site, hybrid or remote) or fully remote in the US.
2. **Comp:** total comp plausibly clears the **$380–400K floor**. Microsoft 65 (~$329K average) is an accepted exception.

Priority among roles that pass: **comp and company first, scope second, title last.** People-manager Director roles at big tech are not a target.

**How to read "Comp gate":**
- **Clears:** evidence of total comp at or above the floor (Levels.fyi average, or posted *base* already near it).
- **Plausible:** some evidence or a strong employer, but not confirmed for this level.
- **Borderline:** evidence puts typical comp near or just under the floor. Apply only to the most senior roles, and ask for the full package at the recruiter screen.
- **Check at screen:** no data. Ask for base + bonus + equity value on the first call.
- LinkedIn's posted ranges are **base salary only**; equity comes on top.
