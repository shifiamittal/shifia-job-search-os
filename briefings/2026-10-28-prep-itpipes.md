> "I start by doing things that don't scale. At Keystone, I spent about three months supporting planners by hand, sitting in their workflows and interviews, to learn demand planning. Later, I designed the agent so it showed its evidence and left high-stakes calls to planners, and 70% of its recommendations were accepted at launch.
